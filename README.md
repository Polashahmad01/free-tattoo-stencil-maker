# Free Online Tattoo Stencil Maker & 300 DPI Thermal Converter

> Convert any sketch, photo, or tattoo concept into a crisp 300 DPI thermal transfer stencil for Brother, Epson, and Spirit carbon paper copiers.

🔗 **Live Tool:** [https://tattoodesignpro.com/tools/stencil-maker](https://tattoodesignpro.com/tools/stencil-maker)  
🌐 **Platform:** [https://tattoodesignpro.com](https://tattoodesignpro.com)

## Features
- **Client-Side Edge Detection:** Fast Sobel contour inversion without uploading private sketches.
- **300 DPI Thermal Ready:** Engineered for thermal copier machines and spirit transfer sheets.
- **Contrast & Blowout Calibration:** Eliminates low-frequency shading to preserve long-term skin healing.
- **3D Anatomical Body Try-On:** Test concepts across 22 body zones with realistic curvature.
